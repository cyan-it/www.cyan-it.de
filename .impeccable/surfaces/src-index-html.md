## Direction contract
THESIS: The homepage is a print-separation proof sheet. Cyan is a process ink laid over a black keyline plate; the page refuses the template hero/icon-card/banner arrangement and puts the case studies before the pitch.
OWN-WORLD: Cool paper ground, ink near-black, process cyan #008acb as fill, deeper cyan #00608f for text and fields. Archivo variable (width and weight axes), self-hosted. Registration targets, crop marks around image plates, a 10-100% cyan tint ramp as the divider and footer bar. No gradients, no shadows, no cards.
STORY: Visitor sees WE ARE CYANTISTS snap into register, sees a real Liebherr plate, understands custom software with proof; then reads cases and quotes, the three offers, and books a Gespraech. Applicants find the hiring sticker in the first view.

Right 4 columns: crane wireframe plate with crop marks, hiring sticker overlapping its lower-left corner. Tint ramp strip under the fold line.
